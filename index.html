Nothing here was picked.

An olfactive atelier. Species are designed as data, run until they hold, and compiled into extrait. Five have been released. The remainder stay in the registry.

Seed

A number and a set of constraints: the hour it opens, the air it opens into, what it may spend energy on. Nothing is inherited from a living plant.

Run

Solved pass after pass until it stops changing, or until we stop it. Most collapse. Surfacing is a separate step, so a species can be known by what it released and never seen at all.

Compile

Each modelled volatile is matched to a material on the bench and weighed against the others. Nothing here yields an oil, so every formula is built the way lilac and lily of the valley have always been built.

The file is the entire plant. There is nothing behind it to cut, press or keep.
Nothing here is extracted. A species is compiled material by material, from what the model says it would have released.
There is no garden to visit. You are the only climate these species will ever grow in.
